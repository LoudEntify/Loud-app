Design system

The rules behind every screen on this canvas

Colour

Ink Black#011627 · all text, dark screens
Porcelain#fdfffc · light screens
Teal#2ec4b6 · main actions, ready
Red#e71d36 · live, errors, stop
Orange#ff9f1c · attention, Versus, tokens
Teal and orange are fills, never text on a light background: both fail contrast against Porcelain at roughly 2:1 where 4.5:1 is needed. Ink text on a teal or orange fill is fine, and so is teal text on Ink.

Surfaces

Light pagePorcelain with soft teal and orange washes
Dark pageVideo screens and anything live
CardWhite at 72% over the page wash
Media tilePlaceholder behind video and covers

Type

PT Sans Narrow, with Arial Narrow as the fallback. Two weights only: 400 and 700. Screen title · 30 to 36 Section heading · 20 to 22 Row title · 17 to 19 Body · 16 to 17 Supporting · 14 to 15, never below 14 LABEL · 13, UPPERCASE, WIDE

Parts

Buttons · 52 high, fully round Main action Strong action Quiet action Destructive
Chips, toggles, badges ChosenNot chosen LIVEVERSUSSOON
Shape and space Corners: 12 on inputs, 14 to 18 on cards, 20 to 24 on panels and sheets, fully round on buttons and chips. Spacing steps: 4, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24. Screen edge is 16 on a phone, 28 to 56 on the web. Nothing tappable under 44 by 44. Icons are stroked at 1.8, in 20, 22, 24 or 26.

Rules that are easy to break by accident

Dark for video, light for everything else. A screen showing a performance is dark. Lists, settings and forms are light.
One main action per screen. If two things look equally important, neither reads as the thing to do.
Red means live or danger. Never use it for decoration, or the LIVE badge stops meaning anything.
Gradients stay soft. They sit behind content, never under small text, and never as a button's only cue.
Write like a person. Short sentences, no jargon, no exclamation marks, and never blame the user.
Respect reduced motion. Every animation, including the launch whale, has a still fallback.
