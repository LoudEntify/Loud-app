One app, four widths

Nothing is redesigned per device. Every screen uses the same parts and switches layout at four widths. The rule is simple: as the screen gets wider, panels that were stacked or hidden come out side by side.

UNDER 600 Phone, and Fold shut
One column. Bottom tabs. Chat and controls sit over the video.
600 TO 839 Fold open, small tablet
Two columns. Tabs become a rail on the left. Chat moves beside the video.
840 TO 1279 Tablet
Rail plus content plus a side panel. Settings show the list and the page together.
1280 AND UP Computer
Full sidebar with labels. This is the web app already designed.

Rules that hold at every width

Video keeps its shape. Empty space gets letterboxed, never stretched or cropped into the performer. Text and tap targets stay the same size. Wider screens show more, not bigger. Reading width caps at about 700px, so settings and legal text never run edge to edge. Main actions stay reachable near a thumb on handhelds, not stranded at the top.

Fold, specifically

Opening or shutting mid-show never interrupts playback. The layout switches, the stream does not restart. Nothing important sits under the crease. Panels split along it instead. Half-open on a table puts the video above the fold and the controls below. A performing artist keeps the same camera and framing across a fold, so viewers see nothing jump.
